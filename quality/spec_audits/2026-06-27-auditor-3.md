# Spec Audit Report — Auditor 3 (Hardware & Power)

## UPS & Power State Review
- **Policy**: System must recover gracefully from power cuts and shut down safely without boot-looping.
- **Audit Findings**:
  - The Raspberry Pi battery monitor loop lacks charging-state check logic.
  - This leads to infinite shutdown cycles during low-battery charging.
- **Verdict**: FAIL.
